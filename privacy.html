Privacy Policy

Access History · Last updated 2026-08-25

This document describes what Access History (the "App") does with data, in plain language. It is written for anyone evaluating the app before installing it on their Jira site.

What the App accesses

When installed on a Jira site, the App reads:

  • The list of projects on the site
  • The project role definitions for each project (e.g. "Administrators," "Developers")
  • The members of each role: display name, and whether the member is a person, a group, or an app/integration account

The App does not read issue content, comments, attachments, or any data outside of project role membership.

What the App stores

Once a week (and on-demand, if an admin clicks "Run snapshot now"), the App saves a snapshot of the above data. Each snapshot is stored using Forge's hosted key-value storage, which runs on Atlassian's own infrastructure — the App does not operate, and does not transmit data to, any server outside of Atlassian's platform. There is no third-party analytics, tracking, or ad service embedded in the App.

Up to roughly two years of weekly snapshots (104) are retained per site; older snapshots are automatically deleted to keep storage bounded. An admin can also export any single snapshot as a CSV file, which downloads directly to their own device — that export is not retained by the App after download.

What happens when the App is uninstalled

Uninstalling the App from a site immediately makes its stored snapshots inaccessible, and the underlying data is permanently deleted from Atlassian's infrastructure within 28 days, per Forge's standard hosted-storage data lifecycle. No copy is retained by the developer.

Who can see this data

Only users with Jira administrator permissions on the site can access the App's admin page, view snapshots, or export a CSV. The App does not expose this data to non-admin users, to other Jira sites, or to the developer — there is no dashboard or backend that receives customer data; everything runs inside Atlassian's Forge platform, scoped to each customer's own site.

Data is never sold or shared

This data is never sold, licensed, or shared with any third party for any purpose, including marketing.
